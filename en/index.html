FAQ

How do I play?
Add the fingers on your hand to an opponent's hand. Drag your hand onto theirs to tap. Play against another person across one iPhone, or choose one of four CPU levels.
What happens above five fingers?
The default is Subtract 5: exactly 5 eliminates the hand, 6 becomes 1 and 7 becomes 2. You can also select elimination at 5 or more, or exactly 5. The exactly-5 rule forbids taps that exceed 5.
Does splitting end my turn?
The default is Split then tap. You may split once, then tap to end your turn. Splitting is optional. You can choose End turn after splitting instead.
Why is there no sound?
Check your iPhone's Silent Mode and volume. The game's sound effects respect Silent Mode.
How do I change my hands?
Open YOUR HANDS in Rules. Random chooses a new appearance each match. Gold hands unlock after 10 wins against Perfect CPU.
Why is Perfect CPU so hard?
Perfect chooses optimal moves for the selected rules. The position and starting side determine whether a win, loss or draw can be forced.
Why are ads not showing?
Ad availability can depend on your connection and consent status. You can keep playing if an ad does not load.
How do I delete a saved rule preset?
Swipe the preset in Save / load rules. Your preferences and victory count are stored on your device.
