Privacy policy

Super Chopsticks Simulator

Effective and last updated: October 17, 2026
Developer: izanapi

1. Data stored in the app

Rule presets, hand appearance, language, Perfect CPU victories and match identifiers used to prevent duplicate victory counts are stored on your device. No account is required. The app does not send this game data to a developer-operated server.

2. Ads and third-party services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) for ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent. These services may process IP addresses, device or ad identifiers, ad views and interactions, usage information, and crash or performance data. An IP address may indicate an approximate location.

These services use information for ad delivery, measurement, improvement and fraud prevention, according to Google's policies, your choices and device settings.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google advertising information
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

3. Ad choices

Where required, the app presents a consent message. When privacy options are available, open Save / load rules, then Ad privacy settings to review your choices. The game remains playable if you decline ad consent or ads are unavailable. You can also review your device's advertising and privacy settings.

4. Support requests

We use the email address and message you provide to respond to your request and investigate problems. Contact us below with privacy questions or requests to delete support information. GitHub Issues are public; do not post personal information there.

5. Support website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no ads or developer-installed analytics. Information processed for hosting or GitHub Issues is covered by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

6. Removing local data

You can delete saved rule presets in the app. Uninstalling removes its locally stored data, although restoring a device backup may restore that data. See the respective service's policy for information processed by Google or GitHub.

We may update this page when features or third-party services change. The current policy and update date are shown here.
